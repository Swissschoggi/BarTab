Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (stored securely by our provider, Supabase).
  • Profile: an optional display name and profile photo.
  • Contributions: prices, bars, ratings and reviews you submit.
  • Location: approximate location, only when you grant permission, to show nearby bars. We do not store a continuous location history.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the core service (finding bars and comparing prices).
  • To show who contributed which prices and ratings.
  • To send price-alert notifications you've opted into.

3. What's public

Prices, bars, ratings and your display name are public and visible to other users that's the point of a crowdsourced app.

4. Third parties

We use Supabase for authentication, storage and the database. We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.

5. Data retention

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. Deleting your account (in the app's Settings) permanently removes your profile and contributions.

6. Contact

Questions? Email us at support@bartap.info.
