Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

We process minimal personal data necessary to provide and improve BarTab:

  • Account Information: Email address and authentication credentials managed securely via Supabase.
  • Location Data: With your permission, precise location data is used exclusively to display nearby venues and verify proximity when submitting prices. Location history is not permanently tracked or mapped to your identity.
  • User Submissions: Prices, drink updates, and venue reports submitted to the platform.

2. How We Use Data

Your data is processed strictly for platform operation, verifying crowdsourced information, facilitating account access, and ensuring app integrity.

3. Data Storage & Third Parties

We utilize reliable cloud infrastructure to host and manage app data:

  • Supabase: For user authentication and database storage under strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.
  • Leaflet & Map Tile Providers: Open-source mapping tools to display venue coordinates without passing personal account identifiers.

4. Your Rights (GDPR & Swiss FADP)

You retain full control over your personal data. You have the right to inspect, export, or request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting support or invoking account deletion in the app settings.

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries or data requests, contact us at support@bartap.info.
